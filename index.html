HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

W A S D swim

E buy / pick up from pedestals

ESC pause   M mute

HOW IT WORKS

Every depth is a randomly generated maze of rooms. Gates seal until you destroy every sea creature.

Cleared rooms drop a glowing UPGRADE ORB - walk into it and choose 1 of 3 upgrades.

TREASURE rooms offer a free weapon. SHOP rooms sell items for coins.

Defeat the boss to open a whirlpool to the next depth. Kill quickly to build a score combo!
